Case of J&K

Article 370 of the Indian Constitution was a temporary provision that granted special autonomous status to the former state of Jammu and Kashmir (J&K). Drafted in Part XXI of the constitution, it allowed the state to have its own constitution, a separate flag, and administrative autonomy over internal governance.💀💀
